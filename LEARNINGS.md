| L11 | A new starter detection signature should not stop at fixtures. The first live sanity run against a real repo is what exposes lexical-overreach noise and forces the boundary to become operationally honest. DS-47 initially treated almost any retained-summary metric mention as in-scope and swept far too much of BMA. The live BMA run forced two tightening moves that the fixtures alone would not have demanded: bound the scan to live report/evidence surfaces instead of archives/work/specs, and require explicit behavior-evidence wording before counting a summary mention as a `D-004` claim. | 2026-04-18 `D-004` repo-auditor starter package. Evidence: `scripts/detect-summary-source-parity-gap.sh`; `tests/test-detect-summary-source-parity-gap.sh`; live DS-47 sanity runs against `/Users/briancl/repos/build-meta-analysis`; and `detection-signatures/DS-43-plus.md`. | (1) After fixture PASS, run the new signature once against a real target before calling the starter package done. (2) Bound starter scans to the evidence surfaces they actually mean to audit. (3) Require claim-language, not just vocabulary overlap, when the detector is about "used as behavior evidence" rather than mere mention. |
| L12 | Command-output ROI gates must scan every governed first-class output they claim to govern, not just the final human report. | L13 | Commit-level ceremony ratios can miss delivery-to-ceremony overload when every commit contains at least one code/test file. | L14 | `set -o pipefail` turns early-exit consumers such as `head` and `grep -q` into large-repo SIGPIPE failures when producers still write after the consumer exits. Bounded sampling and boolean discovery in deterministic audit helpers should use full-drain consumers such as `awk` instead of closing the pipe early. | 2026-05-07 Phase 1 post-merge Vault fixture. Evidence: `stall-risk-score.sh` and `extract-repo-dna.sh` exited 141 on the Vault clone after artifact receipt hardening; `tests/test-large-repo-sigpipe.sh` reproduces the large file-set path. | Avoid `find ... | head` and `find ... | grep -q` in pipefail-enabled helpers that scan large repos. Add large file-set regression coverage when changing deterministic discovery pipelines. |
| L15 | Additive inventory helpers must preserve the existing audit scan budget and prune the active output directory when outputs live inside the target. Otherwise generated receipts/logs can consume the cap or appear as target facts, turning evidence metadata into self-contamination. | 2026-05-08 P5 dual-inventory receipts review. Evidence: `scripts/collect-dual-inventory.py` caps scans at 200 auditor-pruned files, prunes in-target output dirs, and `tests/test-dual-inventory-receipts.sh` covers scan-limited and in-target-output fixtures. | When adding deterministic receipt collectors, enforce repo-level scan limits, sort traversal deterministically, prune generated output paths, and test both cap and in-target-output cases. |
